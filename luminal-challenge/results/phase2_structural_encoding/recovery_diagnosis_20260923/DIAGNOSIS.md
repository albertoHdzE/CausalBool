were no INTERRUPTED rows and no stream was marked incomplete. Across the 80,000
raw-bit attempts, 75,973 were INVALID_CODE (a fixed-width structural rank was
outside the legal prefix option list) and 4,027 reached a prefix with no legal

DEAD_END and 819 were COMPLETE. Every option-path choice was a member of its
current legal option list, so those dead ends are locally legal prefixes with
no remaining in-horizon completion. They are not validator discrepancies.
There were zero case failures and zero discrepancies.

## Bootstrap and codec verification

For each of the eight public programs, the unchanged direct bootstrap was
reconstructed in the same whole-program domain. It passed machine validation
and every pinned public case. Its normalized compilation was encoded and
decoded with absolute, static_rank, vector_block and structural_rank. All four
decoders returned COMPLETE, matched normalized identity, canonically
re-encoded the same index, and agreed on C, S and J. Per-program values and
case counts are in DIAGNOSIS.json. This rules out an incumbent/round-trip defect
as the cause of the historical coverage failure.

The failed original attempts are expected under the frozen sampler. Raw bits
mostly hit ranks outside a short legal option list; option paths never select
an illegal current option, but can choose schedules whose later operations
have no remaining issue cycle. The checker independently replayed the same
seeds and reported zero findings. No decoder, validator, case, deadline or
accounting defect is implicated by the retained evidence.
