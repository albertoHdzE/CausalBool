## Evidence classes (keep them apart in the paper)

The third round produced four kinds of evidence. Each one supports a different
strength of claim.

1. **Original registered result (historical).**
   - The M gate registered in the parent run gave a conservative predicted ratio
     of **0.8268**, against a threshold of 0.80. It stopped the round with
     **NO_JUSTIFIED_MECHANISM** (`third_round_20260925/MECHANISM_DECISION.json`).
   - It is retained, but it cannot support rejecting the mechanism, because its
     `O` term had a cost-scope defect (lead review).
2. **Post-hoc development accounting repair (lead-approved).**
   - The correction:
     - `O` uses the scope-matched minimum;
     - the adapter was re-measured with a real skeleton (90 rows).
   - The recalibrated conservative ratio is **0.7864** (point prediction 0.6061).
   - Families: scalar 0.831, vector 0.721, mixed 0.727, dependency 0.900,
     aliasing 0.767.
   - This is a development forecast on 30 development programs. It is not a
     compile-call measurement or a confidence bound (`RECALIBRATED_PREDICTION.json`,
     `REPAIR_CLOSURE.json`).
3. **Kernel-only evidence.**
   - BS1 replays R0's captured propagation work identically: outputs, per-call
     certificate counts and the certificate stream on 30/30 workloads (829,993
     events).
   - The replay-only speedup has a median of **2.3649**, range 0.920–4.361. The
     parent's figure of 2.3948 was an upper median (`SPEEDUP_ADDENDUM.md`).
   - This is not a compiler speedup.
4. **Real compiler results (C1 = R0 + BS1 in the expander, one candidate).**
   - Stage D ran on 100 development programs. Stage C ran on a fresh 200-program
     cohort (seeds 980000–980199, first generated in this stage).

## Limitations to state

- **Exploratory development:** the mechanism was developed and selected on
  development seeds 800000–800099. The kernel draft preceded its specification,
  and the parent cost model was corrected after its outcome. The lead admitted
  the correction as development accounting; the fresh confirmation (seeds
  980000–980199) is the only inferential evidence.
- **Local machine only:** there is no private grader, no general complexity or
  asymptotic claim, and no claim that the method runs faster than classical
  compilation.
- **What the result establishes:** the speedup is an engineering property of
  this Python implementation. A child reuses the facts derived at its parent,
  and the index-set method's decisions are unchanged. It does not establish a
  new algorithmic complexity class, and it does not establish novelty.
- **Process deviations** (see `RELEASE_HANDOFF.md`, D1–D8): an auditor append
  after the D freeze, a false-positive inventory corrected before the freeze,
  a lint-only unused variable in a frozen file, and an order difference in D.
