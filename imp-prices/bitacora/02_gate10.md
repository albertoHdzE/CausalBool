## 1. The question, and why it needed decomposing

Gate 1.0 asks whether the GWP3-discretised panel contains deterministic structure
that a gate-based model could find. Written that way the question is
under-specified in a way that would have produced a false positive, and the
controls exposed it before the market was measured.

The target's own lagged regime is one of the seven candidate parents. Persistence
is therefore *inside* the hypothesis space — and persistence is already anchor
A11, the 79.31 per cent benchmark that the GWP3 belief network could not beat. An
undecomposed gate would have detected the benchmark and reported it as a finding.
The gate was therefore split into four blocks (self, cross, macro-only,
unrestricted) and given a fifth, decisive test: the increment over persistence.

## 2. Two traps, named before they were hit

**Vacuous determinism.** Seven ternary variables give a 2,187-state input space
and the training window supplies 138 observations. Measured on the full pattern,
the contradiction rate would be near zero because nothing recurs — absence of
data reported as perfect determinism. Every statistic is therefore accompanied by
its recurrence, candidates below 50 per cent recurrence are excluded identically
under the real data and under every surrogate, and `pattern_stats` returns `nan`
rather than zero when nothing recurs. That last point is asserted as a test.

**Selection over parent sets.** The reported statistic is a best-of over 63
candidates and is biased by that search. The null is therefore best-of-search
under surrogates, over the identical candidate list. Comparing a searched
statistic against an unsearched null is the artefact Level 2 caught and reported;
it is not repeated here.

## 3. The control that condemned my own null

This is the substantive methodological event of the session and it is worth
recording in full, because the first version of the gate would have passed.

The pre-registered null (§2 as frozen) was a random permutation of the successor
column. It preserves the regime marginal — the 66.4 per cent stagnant share that
dominates this target — and destroys the correspondence between an evidence row
and its successor.

It also destroys the successor's **autocorrelation**, and that is fatal. Two
persistent but wholly independent processes align spuriously over a finite
sample; this is the categorical analogue of spurious regression. A permutation
null cannot reproduce the alignment, so it certifies it as structure.

The `persistent_random_frame` control — seven independent Markov chains, each
staying put with probability 0.75, containing no cross-variable structure
whatsoever by construction — was written to test exactly this, and it failed:

```
persistent random, cross only    permutation null   p = 0.0050
```

A false positive on a system built to have nothing in it. Had the gate run
without that control, the panel's cross-variable result would have been reported
as structure on the strength of the same defect.

The remedy is a **circular shift**: rotate the successor column against the
evidence. This preserves the marginal, the autocorrelation, the run-length
distribution and the regime clustering *exactly*, and destroys only the
alignment. On the same control:

```
persistent random, cross only    circular-shift null  p = 0.4921  (excess +0.0026)
persistent random, any parents   circular-shift null  p = 0.0053  (excess +0.1474)
```

Null on cross-variable structure, positive on the genuine persistence in the same
data. That is the discrimination the analyser has to make, and both directions
are now asserted in the suite — including the permutation null's failure, so that
if it ever stops failing, the documentation is known to be wrong.

The surrogate set is enumerated exhaustively (126 shifts, excluding the six at
either end where the alignment survives), so the null carries no seed and the
result is deterministic. The attainable *p*-value floor is 1/127 = 0.0079.

This is the fourth artefact-class catch in the programme: trend contamination at
Level 4, the fat-tail driver at Level 5, the one-point causality check in bitácora
01, and now the permutation null. In every case a control caught what intuition
did not.

## 4. The contradiction rate is saturated here

The pre-registered statistic does not work at this alphabet size. With three
successor values, ten to fifteen recurring patterns and multiplicities up to 44,
almost every recurring pattern carries more than one successor whatever the data.
The best observed value is 0.70, and it sits *above* its own null — the real data
are, by this measure, marginally *less* deterministic than surrogates. The
statistic is not wrong; it is uninformative in this regime.

It is retained and reported, and it remains decisive where recurrence is high and
the alphabet small: rule 110 returns exactly 0.0000. Lookup accuracy — the
in-sample accuracy of the majority table — becomes the operative statistic, read
only ever against the null, since it is optimistic by construction.

## 5. The measurement

Coverage first, because it conditions everything: 138 observations over 2,187
states, **34 distinct states visited (1.55 per cent), 15 recurring, maximum
multiplicity 44**. Recurrence at the parent-set level is nevertheless high
(k = 1: 1.000, k = 2: 0.997, k = 3: 0.991), so per-parent-set statistics are
interpretable even though whole-pattern statistics are not. That is precisely the
factorisation the method relies on.

| Block | Lookup accuracy | Shift null | Excess | *p* |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| self (persistence) | 0.7737 | 0.6645 ± 0.0016 | **+0.1092** | 0.0079 (floor) |
| cross (six others) | 0.8029 | 0.7024 ± 0.0204 | **+0.1005** | 0.0079 (floor) |
| macro only | 0.6788 | 0.6798 ± 0.0165 | −0.0010 | 0.5118 |
| any | 0.8102 | 0.7067 ± 0.0194 | **+0.1035** | 0.0079 (floor) |

Base rate 0.6642. The three significant rows beat every one of the 126
surrogates.

Read naively this says: strong structure, gate passes, build the network. Two
things say otherwise.

**Contemporaneous agreement.** WTI\_CL shares the target's decoded regime in 87.7
per cent of months and Brent in 79.0 per cent, against 11.6 to 30.4 per cent for
the four macroeconomic series. The "cross" block is largely the target under
another name — the same barrel, priced on a different contract.

**Macro-only is exactly null.** Excess −0.0010, *p* = 0.512. Whatever the cross
block found, the macroeconomic variables contributed none of it.

## 6. The decisive test: does anything add to persistence?

A raw comparison cannot answer this — enlarging a parent set can only raise a
lookup table's in-sample accuracy, so the increment is positive by construction.
The covariate-shift null rotates the *added* columns while leaving the target,
the successor and the persistence structure aligned. The surrogate increment is
then the increment attributable to nothing, and the comparison is like for like.

| Added to the target's own lag | Increment | Surrogate increment | Excess | *p* |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| all six other series | +0.0365 | +0.0292 ± 0.0110 | +0.0073 | 0.323 |
| the two oil futures | +0.0219 | +0.0191 ± 0.0120 | +0.0028 | 0.504 |
| the four macro series | +0.0146 | +0.0149 ± 0.0097 | −0.0003 | 0.638 |
| *control: a true leading indicator* | *+0.1759* | *+0.0008* | *+0.1751* | *0.0053* |

**Nothing adds to persistence.** The test has power — it finds a genuine leading
indicator at excess +0.1751 — and it has size — an unrelated covariate produces a
positive raw increment and a *p* above 0.05, which is asserted as a test.

## 7. Verdict and what it explains

Gate 1.0 **passes as literally worded** and **fails on its stated intent**. Both
readings are in the ledger; C9 and C10 are the operative results.

The value of this is not the negative itself. It is what the negative explains.
GWP3 recorded two facts about its improved belief network: it was statistically
indistinguishable from persistence (McNemar *p* = 1.00, anchor A12), and its
Markov blanket collapsed to WTI\_CL alone (anchor A10). Both were reported as
properties of the fitted model, and the report's §7 treats the blanket collapse
as an instability to be apologised for.

C9 and C10 show that both are properties of **the data**. There is no predictive
content for the one-month WTI regime in this panel beyond the regime's own
persistence, for any model over these seven variables at in-degree ≤ 3. The
blanket collapsed to WTI\_CL because WTI\_CL is the target under another name.
The network tied persistence because persistence is everything there is.

This converts a reported failure into a measured explanation, and it does so by a
route entirely independent of the belief network — no scoring function, no
conditional probability table, no hill climbing. That is the first thing the
index-set calculus has contributed here that the probabilistic machinery could
not.

## 8. Consequence for the phases

**Phase 1 forecasting comparison: not attempted**, per protocol §2. Running the
drop-in as a contest would measure noise, and whatever accuracy it produced would
be a restatement of persistence dressed as a result.

**Phase 1 method comparison: retained.** B4 (description length of the two
encodings) and B5 (bootstrap stability of functional connectivity against the
belief network's unstable edge set, anchor A15) are statements about the method
on a fixed target, not claims of predictive skill, and they remain both
meaningful and cheap.

**Phase 2: proceeds as pre-registered, and is now better motivated than before.**
The panel has no cross-variable content for *direction* at monthly frequency.
That is the fourth independent confirmation of the direction negative in the
wider programme, and it is exactly the premise on which the clock re-target was
designed. The one caution to carry forward: the target here is 66 per cent
stagnant and persistence-dominated, which is precisely the base-rate pathology
Phase 2 exists to escape.

## 9. Honest residuals

- The gate tests in-degree ≤ 3 over seven ternary variables. It does not exclude
  structure at higher in-degree, at longer lags, or under a different
  discretisation. Coverage at 1.55 per cent means the sample cannot support the
  first; the second and third are Phase 2 and Phase 3 questions. The negative is
  scoped to what was tested and is not a claim about oil markets in general.
- Lookup accuracy is an in-sample quantity. It is interpretable here only because
  every comparison is against a surrogate computed the same way. It must not be
  quoted as a forecasting accuracy anywhere.
- 126 surrogates put a floor of 0.0079 under every *p*-value. The three
  significant blocks are at that floor, meaning they beat every surrogate; the
  floor is a limit on resolution, not a marginal result.

determinism is verified by comparing one hexadecimal string rather than by trusting a
diff. Two consecutive runs: `391284c16db84c66…`, identical.
