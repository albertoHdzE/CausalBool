# Level 17 — Universal clock law (data collapse)

**Purpose.** Test whether the market clock obeys ONE universal law rather than a
model per stock: rescale each stock's gaps by its own mean and ask whether all
100 normalised distributions collapse.

**Key results (qualitative).** (1) The gap distribution is universal and
collapses; (2) the universal shape is marginal, not structural (the honest
catch); (3) the universal structural constant is the exponent α.
