## 1. What was tested

Bitacora 33 framed one hypothesis: a fixed inference procedure using index-set schemata
and compositional rules can discover descriptions that reconstruct unseen binary
sequences exactly and use fewer transmitted bits than specified baselines, on specified
families. HID-v1 makes every part of that sentence concrete:

* **a format** (`ISD1` archives, ten codecs) in which literals, ordered concatenation,
  repetition, arithmetic-progression supports, schema supports (mask/value cubes over an
  explicit, clipped address domain), exact XOR patches and fixed transforms are
  transmitted field by field, and an **independent decoder** (standard library, no shared
  parsing) that reads nothing but the archive;
* **a bounded, deterministic search** that receives the bit string and a frozen
  configuration, never a family, seed, period, mask or file name, and chooses only by the
  length of the complete archive, with the raw literal always a candidate;
* **a benchmark frozen before its data existed** (freeze sha256 `ba4bec0a…`, written 2026-10-02T15:06:52Z UTC): 1,440 confirmation strings from twelve families (four held out of
  development entirely), 192 transfer strings of 16,387–65,539 bits, fifteen encodings
  per string, and the decodable best of nine real baselines (raw, RLE, gaps, period,
  Bernoulli-enumerative, first-order context-enumerative, zlib, xz, a RePair-style pair
  grammar) as the comparator.

The measured quantity is **archive bits**: 8 × the bytes of a complete archive including
magic, codec byte, length fields and padding. It is a code length in one fixed language
under one search budget — not K, not CTM/BDM, not an entropy.

## 2. Result

**The prespecified advantage is rejected on the structured confirmation population.** The
equal-weight mean saving is negative and its 95 % interval lies entirely below zero
(table below). The engineering outcome is clean: every one of 26,112 method rows is
present and `ok`, every stored archive decodes to its regenerated input, there were no
timeouts, no RSS breaches and no errors, and `verify --full` exits 0.

What the descriptive tables show, and what they do not:

* **Where HID wins.** Arithmetic supports (F04) at every confirmation and transfer size,
  and noisy periods (F06) at 256 and 1,024 bits, where a period plus an exact patch list
  is cheaper than any baseline. Schema supports (F05) are near zero: positive at the
  transfer sizes, slightly negative at confirmation sizes.
* **Where it loses, and why** (diagnosed from stored archives; nothing was changed after
  freezing): on plain periods (F01) the period codec's single field undercuts HID's
  REPEAT + tail + CONCAT records; on F06 at 4,096 bits and above, the 128 or more
  flips exceed the frozen 64-flip cap for a whole-string patch, so only patches of
  sub-blocks remain (the single largest success, an F06 string at 4,096 bits, is built
  from exactly such sub-block patches, but the route is not found reliably); on mixed
  regimes (F12) HID finds the periodic regions on fixed grids and even relates chunks by
  rotation, but zlib's sliding window is not tied to a grid; on statistical controls
  (F07–F09) the language has no entropy coding by design. Reference streams cost at
  least one byte per symbol, which also explains the F02 losses.
* **Components.** Each of the five restricted arms is worse than the full search by an
  amount whose 99 % interval excludes zero; the largest effect is `flat` (no nesting, no
  sharing). Hierarchy and the other components therefore help HID relative to restricted
  versions of itself — but not enough to beat the portfolio. The ablation results must not
  be read as an advantage over the baselines.
* **Transfer** (descriptive) shows the same pattern at 16,384 and 65,536 bits: F04 and
  F05 positive, every other family negative or tied.
* **BDM diagnostics.** With setting selection calibrated symmetrically on 199
  marginal-preserving shuffles per object, six of the seven structured objects are
  separated from their shuffles after Holm correction, F04 is not, so the diagnostic
  claim is recorded as inconclusive. F09 is also separated; the shuffle null preserves
  only bit marginals, so that is no evidence of structure beyond its Markov generator.

## 4. H0 corrections (shared owner and notebook 15)

`src/description_lengths.py` now validates 1-D inputs before conversion (floats —
including 0.5, which the old `dtype=int` cast silently turned into 0 — non-binary,
ragged, empty and scalar data are refused), requires integral `block`/`shift`, refuses
unknown remainder policies and short inputs under `raise`/`drop`, adds
`remainder="recursive"` (full coverage) and `bdm_1d_partition` (coverage reported), and
adds `encoded_bit_length`. Previously valid scores are unchanged: 0.0 maximum difference
over 20,458 comparisons against the starting copy. Notebook 15 was corrected through its
builder (names A64/A72/A24/AX64/AXM64; characters vs UTF-8 bits vs archive bits; raw BDM
minimum selects b = 3 on A72; P3 has 15 distinct of 16 blocks; logarithmic bound on
fixed-b BDM; invariance constants are not error bars; full-coverage scans beside the
historical drop policy; the period/schema distinction) and re-executed with zero errors.
Bitacora 32 carries dated errata; its measured tables and its incomplete quotation were
not rewritten.

## 5. What this does not show

No claim of Kolmogorov complexity, unique generator recovery, prediction of unseen
suffixes, causal identification, or priority of hierarchical grammar coding follows from
this study (claims C7–C10 are out of scope). "Best found under budget" is the only
optimality statement; the schema cover it calls is greedy. A different reference coding,
larger caps, or a region-discovery operator might change the sign — that would be a new,
separately frozen study on fresh seeds, not a reinterpretation of this one.

## 6. Smallest warranted next experiment

Keep the frozen language and change one thing whose effect the development data already
isolates: entropy-code the reference stream (or allow a statistical leaf codec inside the
graph) and rerun the same benchmark design on **new reserved seeds**, disclosing that
`confirm-v1` informed the change.
