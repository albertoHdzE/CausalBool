# Level 15 — Blind play (out-of-sample)

**Purpose.** Play the buy/sell behaviour formulae blindly on held-out time for
one stock and the 100-stock panel, scoring TIMING and MONEY separately, each
against its own control.

**Key results (qualitative).** Timing works — weakly but for real (against the
shuffle, across sectors); money does not (the ceiling, made visible). This is
the verdict and the green light for the fusion programme.
