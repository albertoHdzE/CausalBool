  pruning. Retain a mapping from baseline symbols to the resulting HID graph.
  No grammar rerun, optimization search or metadata-derived extra rules.

`H - C = (H - T) + (T - C)`.

HID proposal is impossible, or establish a causal decomposition of the primary
loss. Do not add these terms across different diagnostic populations to explain
