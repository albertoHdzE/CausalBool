## 1. What was built

The isolated owner `isolated/index-deconvolution/src/deconvolution.py` gains three
functions: `minimal_task_partition`, `distinguishing_task_word` and `task_word_path`.
Together they compute the coarsest grouping of ALL states that preserves a named
observable h now and after every finite word of declared actions. The method is the
classical layer-wise refinement (Knuutila 2001, Proposition 4), with the previous label
kept in each signature. The functions reuse the owner's `canonical_partition`,
`induced_map` and `commutation_failures`. The proof of exactness, minimality and
uniqueness up to labels, including the all-start difference from a connected DFA, is in
`THEORY.md`. No novelty is claimed. The result is exact sufficiency relative to the
supplied transition tables; it is not causal identification from passive data.

## 3. Results (24 cells; full table in REPORT_TABLES.md)

**Primary, INTERVENTION (12 cells):** EXACT_REDUCTION 3, NO_REDUCTION 9.

- M2/T0: K* = 2 of N = 256. The counter's bit 0 depends only on bit 0 under every one of
  the 42 actions (the 3-bit analogue is fixture FX7).
- M4/T0: K* = 2 of N = 1024. v000 is a self-loop, so the 52 actions cannot make the
  next value of bit 0 depend on any other bit.
- M4/T1: K* = 16 of N = 1024, with K0 = 2. The grouping depends exactly on bits
  {0, 1, 8, 9}. This is the closed upstream chain v000 → v001 → v008 → v009 of the
  EGFR file, and 2^4 = 16. Interventions on nodes 2–7 cannot reach it.
- The other nine primary cells have K* = N: every state must be kept. These cells are
  M1 T0/T1/TP, M2 T1/TP, M3 T0/T1/TP and M4 TP.

**Controls, AUTO (12 cells):** EXACT_REDUCTION 10, NO_REDUCTION 2 (M2 T1 and M2 TP,
K* = 256 of 256).

- M1 (rule 150, linear over GF(2)): T0 and T1 give K* = 16 of 256. TP gives K* = 2,
  because parity is conserved, as in fixture FX8.
- M3 (rule 30): T0 and T1 give K* = 245 of 256; TP gives 29 of 256.
- M4: T1 gives 16 and TP gives 202, both of 1024.

**Adding actions:** in all 12 INTERVENTION/AUTO pairs, the INTERVENTION optimum refines
the AUTO optimum (audit, `intervention_refines_auto`). Adding the interventions removed
every AUTO reduction in M1, M3 and M4/TP. It left M2/T0, M4/T0 and M4/T1 unchanged.

**Refinement depth:** strict rounds range from 0 to 127. The 127 rounds are M2/T1/AUTO,
whose chain grows by two blocks per round, from 2 to 256. Depth d means "outputs agree
for every word of at most d actions". Intermediate stages are horizon refinements, not
closed models.

A gap is a state-cardinality statement only. It does not imply an archive or
description-length saving.

## 5. What the capacity numbers are and are not

n − ceil(log2 K*) counts integer state bits only. It excludes the state-to-group map
(N entries), the decoder (K*), the macro tables (|Q|·K*) and the action labels. For
example, in M4/T1/INTERVENTION the capacity saving is 6 bits of 10. Against that, the
model keeps a 1,024-entry map and 52 × 16 = 832 macro-table entries (`REPORT_TABLES.md`).
These capacity numbers are not archive compression, total memory or runtime savings, and
they do not license any claim about grammar, self-similarity or spatial scale.
